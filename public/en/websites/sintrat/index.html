The project

A digital presence
serving the institution.

SINTRAT represents road transport, technical assistance and related workers in Mozambique. Its website presents the institution, union services and membership information.

In 2025, Inbytes developed the SINPOCAF, SINPEOC and SINTRAT websites under a contract awarded by the International Transport Federation.

What the website offers

Clear communication.
Bringing institutions closer.

01

Institutional identity

An introduction to the union, the sectors it represents and its principles.

02

Services for members

Information about legal and employment assistance, training and collective bargaining.

03

Joining the union

Information on registration steps and access to union benefits.

04

Contact and activities

Contact channels, an enquiry form and event information.
