Caderneta Online is an Inbytes product that brings academic information together, allowing schools, parents and guardians to follow students' progress online.

What you can do

The essentials of school life.
All in one place.

01

Academic records

Record grades, averages, absences and homework from a phone or computer.

02

Alerts for parents and guardians

Share assessment dates, results and school-recorded incidents by phone or email.

03

Individual monitoring

View students' performance, upcoming assessments and homework records.

04

Grade sheets and reports

View academic information and generate and print grade sheets by class or student.

05

Online access

Individual access with a username and password to view the information available to each user profile.

06

Adapted to your school

Adapt the solution to each school's processes and specific needs.

How it works

From the classroom
to parents and guardians.

01

The school records

Teachers or school staff select the class and record assessments, grades, absences and homework.

02

Information reaches families

Parents and guardians receive alerts about records concerning their children.

03

Everyone stays informed

Parents and guardians follow academic progress, while the school office monitors students and issues grade sheets.
