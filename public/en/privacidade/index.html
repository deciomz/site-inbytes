Privacy and contact.

Information about data submitted through this website.

The data you send us

The form collects your name, email address, organisation when provided, subject and message. We use this information to understand and respond to your enquiry.

Form delivery and protection

Messages are forwarded to Inbytes by email. Delivery uses Resend and spam protection uses Cloudflare Turnstile. These services are involved in the technical processing of your enquiry.

The website applies sending limits to reduce abuse. Do not submit passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information through this form.

Language preference

The Portuguese and English versions have their own URLs. Switching language takes you to the corresponding page.

Questions about your data

To ask about how we use the data you submitted, or request its correction or deletion, contact Inbytes at info@inbytes.co.mz.
