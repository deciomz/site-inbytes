Questions about this service

Do I need an app or a website?

It depends on the tasks and frequency of use. A mobile-friendly website may be sufficient; an app may make sense when device-specific needs exist. Analysis helps determine the right option.

How long does app development take?

The timeline depends on scope, integrations, content and validation. After assessing requirements, the proposal sets out the stages and schedule.
