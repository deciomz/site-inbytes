Questions about this service

Existing product or a custom system?

We assess the need first. An existing product may cover established processes; custom development requires requirements, integrations and maintenance to be defined. The proposal explains the approach.

Can it integrate with other systems?

Integration depends on the interfaces, permissions and technical conditions of the platforms involved. We assess feasibility before committing to delivery.
